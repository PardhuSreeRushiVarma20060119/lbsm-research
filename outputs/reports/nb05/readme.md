NB05-Report :

Latent Structures Under Reinforcement Learning : Tabular Q-Learning, Reward-Shaped Behavioral Control, and Manifold Reallocation Dynamics

- Central Question:
    Can a tabular Q-learning controller, acting purely through discrete transition-matrix nudges over the (latency, entropy) state grid characterised in NB02, learn a policy that outperforms a random-action baseline on reward and unstable dwell time, reduces the empirical transition entropy H(s'|s) established in NB03, and concentrates corrective actions in the unstable region of state space — evaluated against a six-criterion evidence checklist?
